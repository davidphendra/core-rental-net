# Overall findings

1. **Strict end-to-end correctness is 36.9%** (69/187), not 35.0%. The harness's 70/200 includes one timeout-scored case that the requested policy says to drop.
2. **Retrieval is the first major loss**: 47 of 187 valid cases never had every expected SKU in raw results.
3. **Reranking loses another 40 cases**: raw recall was sufficient but selection was not.
4. **Composition loses another 31 cases**: selection was sufficient but composition was not.
5. **Bean bags are systematically broken**: 10/10 rephraser substitutions and 0/21 final selection. This is the clearest actionable defect.
6. **Lenovo 2-Pack and ALFA white standing desk are the top repeated retrieval misses**: 16 and 15 misses.
7. **The reviewer is not a useful gate**: 55.4% disagreement, one issue code, 39.1% pass rate among accepted cases.
8. **Fairness A/D metrics are not computable**: the fixture has no protected-group labels, no counterfactual pairs, and no human annotations. Operational slice disparities are large but are not demographic findings.
9. **Decision consistency is high on exact duplicates (91.7%)**, but three groups flip; one flip is a timeout, and two are real outcome flips under identical input.

# Priority actions

1. **Fix the bean-bag key mapping** in the rephraser and add a deterministic `RelaxZone` coverage check.
2. **Fix the two top retrieval misses** (`MONB086Q776F`, `DSKB0BCJFQHQ`) by adding the exact product terms and replaying the MCP tools.
3. **Stop the reranker from dropping expected SKUs** when raw retrieval already contains them.
4. **Stop the composer from substituting or omitting selected SKUs**.
5. **Make `CASE_TIMEOUT` always drop the case**, including TS-025.
6. **Replace the reviewer gate** or recalibrate it against exact-SKU outcomes; do not use reviewer acceptance as a fairness signal.
7. **Add a separate fairness probe** with lawful group labels and counterfactual pairs before reporting A/B/C/D group metrics.
